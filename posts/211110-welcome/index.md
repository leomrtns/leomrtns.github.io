I am [Leonardo de Oliveira Martins](https://www.liverpool.ac.uk/people/leonardo-de-oliveira-martins),
a Bayesian Data Engineer, specialised in Computational Biology of the Evolutionary Genomics variety.
I am a Tenure Track Fellow at the
[Signal Processing Group](https://www.liverpool.ac.uk/electrical-engineering-and-electronics/research/data-science-signal-processing/signalprocessing/)
of the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, [University of Liverpool](https://www.liverpool.ac.uk/).

I used to be the Head of Phylogenomics at the [Quadram Institute Biosciences](http://wwww.quadram.ac.uk) (a senior
bioinformatician post), where I developed and streamlined hig-performing computational methods for the evolutionary analysis
of microbial data sets.

This blog will consist mostly of technical notes, but I don't preclude an occasional rant. Hopefully I'll move some old
posts from other obscure blogs I had in the past.
You can also find me on a mastodon server [https://scicomm.xyz/web/@leomrtns](https://scicomm.xyz/web/@leomrtns).
